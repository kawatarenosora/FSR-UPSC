File-level state in FSR-UPSC: this mirror is byte-identical to the jar's
shaders/fsr3/*.glsl, EXCEPT our own M3 addition in DEPTH_CLIP_CS.glsl
(uReactiveMask input, one block, marked "FSR-UPSC M3"). The Minecraft
sky/cloud heuristics remain as published and are gated at runtime by
host-supplied uniforms (uUseDepth=0 by default), not by editing the math
(see docs/PHASE2-FSR3-GLSLDESIGN.md section 1.5).

Frame generation: the jar also contains FG passes (FG_DENOISE /
FG_DISOCCLUSION_MASK / FG_GAME_MV_FIELD / FG_INTERPOLATION); they are NOT
mirrored or bundled by FSR-UPSC. fsr-reloaded itself drives FG through
AMD's signed native DLLs shipped in that jar
(amd_fidelityfx_framegeneration_dx12.dll and friends), which FSR-UPSC does
not redistribute. Our own frame generation is a pure-GL double-present
implementation (src/fsrupsc/FgPresent.java) with no AMD/upstream code.
